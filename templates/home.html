About This Application

The CoI (Cause of Incident) system is designed to streamline incident management processes for teams. It provides tools for tracking incidents, assigning action items, and managing team collaboration.

Key features include:

  • Incident tracking and management
  • Action item assignment and monitoring
  • Team collaboration tools
  • Administrative controls
  • Flexible authentication options

Whether you're managing a small team or a large organization, CoI provides the tools needed to keep incidents under control and ensure timely resolution.

Incident Management

Track and manage incidents from detection through resolution with detailed reporting and status updates.

Action Items

Assign and track action items with clear ownership and deadlines to ensure follow-through.

Team Collaboration

Enable seamless collaboration between team members with shared workspaces and communication tools.
